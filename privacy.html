KACHUNK privacy notice

Who is responsible

Jordi Valladares Gay is responsible for KACHUNK. You can reach us at dontworkstudios@gmail.com.

Local play

KACHUNK saves your settings, progress and personal Daily best in your browser or desktop app. You can clear a browser save in your browser’s site data settings. itch.io handles visits to its site, file downloads and purchases under its own policy. itch.io privacy policy

Optional world leaderboard

You can play the full game and keep a local best without joining the leaderboard. If you choose to submit a Daily result, we receive your selected nickname, score and Daily date, plus a random installation ID so we can keep your best entry. We receive your moves and the puzzle version to check that the moves produce the submitted score. We record when we accept an entry. The leaderboard sorts scores automatically, without profiling players or making other automated decisions about them. The public table displays your nickname, score and date.

We use your consent to enter and display your score. We use our legitimate interest in preventing abuse to verify scores and protect the service. We use leaderboard data only to run and protect the ranking.

The leaderboard is for players aged 14 or older. Before joining, the game asks you to confirm your age with one tap. If you're younger, skip it: the game works the same without it.

Hosting and retention

The leaderboard is hosted by Cloudflare. Entries are stored in the European Union. Cloudflare can process requests on its global network, including outside the EEA. Its Data Processing Addendum describes the safeguards for these transfers, including the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and EU Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable. Cloudflare Data Processing Addendum

Cloudflare can receive your IP address and request time for network security. We keep IP addresses out of the leaderboard database and disable Workers Logs for this service. When enabled, Cloudflare’s published Workers Logs retention on the Free plan is three days through November 2026 and seven days from December 1, 2026. Cloudflare does not publish a fixed period for its own security logs for this service. Workers Logs, Observability pricing, and Cloudflare privacy policy

Leaderboard entries are kept for 30 days after their Daily, then deleted. Submitted moves are used only to check the score and are not kept after that. Deleted entries may remain in Cloudflare’s rolling recovery backups for up to seven days. When you delete your scores, we keep a day-specific hash until the next UTC reset to prevent a pending submission from restoring your entries. Cloudflare D1 recovery window

Your choices and rights

Use Delete my scores in Settings to remove your leaderboard entries. You can withdraw your consent at any time without affecting processing that has already taken place.

Email dontworkstudios@gmail.com to ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of your data, or to object where the law allows. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency. AEPD
